Frequently asked questions

I'm moving to a new phone, or I need to delete the app
Deleting the app deletes your records too. First go to Settings → Move your records → Copy, and paste the text into a note or a message to yourself. On the new phone (or after reinstalling), copy that text and tap Move your records → Import.

No letters are arriving
The first letter arrives after you spend a few minutes in the village. After that, a letter comes once a day when you visit, and a reply comes shortly after you send a letter. You can read past letters under Letters → Inbox.

I want to change my dog's details
You can change the name, breed, coat colour and more in the "My dog" tab at the bottom.

I want to change the language
Choose 한국어, English, 日本語 or 中文 under Settings → Language.

I get a "harmful app" warning when installing or opening
Your phone's security features may warn about installer files that did not come from a store. Please get Cloud Village from Google Play or the App Store.
