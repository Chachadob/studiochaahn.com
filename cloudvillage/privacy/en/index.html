Effective: November 3, 2026

STUDIO CHAAHN ("the Studio", "we") provides the mobile game Cloud Village ("the game"). This policy is given under the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws.

1. Personal information we collect: none

The game does not collect your personal information.

  • There is no sign-up, login or account.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • There are no analytics tools and no crash-reporting tools.
  • There is no game server. The game works without an internet connection and never sends your game records off your device.
  • It does not access your advertising ID, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Data stored only on your device

What you type in order to play is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Studio or anyone else.

  • Your dog's details: name, breed, coat colour, age, birthday and the day they crossed the rainbow bridge (if you enter them), favourite snacks and toys, habits, and the name your dog called you
  • Letters you write, letters you receive from your dog, your progress and settings (language, sound and so on)

This data is removed from your device when you delete the app or choose "Start over" in the game settings. We cannot see it or recover it.

3. Moving your records

"Move your records → Copy" in Settings turns the records above into text and copies it to your device's clipboard. Where you paste or send that text is up to you, and we never receive it. It contains your dog's details and your letters, so keep it only somewhere you trust.

4. Sharing, outsourcing and international transfers

None. We do not provide or entrust your information to any other company, and we do not transfer it abroad. App installs and updates are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under Google's privacy policy or Apple's privacy policy.

5. If you email us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and message in order to reply. We use them only to reply and delete them within one year after your enquiry is resolved.

6. Children

Anyone, including children, can play the game. Because we collect no personal information from any user, we collect none from children. The story touches on saying goodbye to a beloved dog, so we suggest that children play together with a parent or guardian. Children under 14 should contact us together with a parent or guardian.

7. Automatic collection tools

The game does not use cookies, advertising IDs or device identifiers.

8. Your rights

You (or a legal guardian, for users under 14) can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you (email enquiries). Email us at the address below and we will tell you the result within 10 days. You can change or delete the game records stored on your device yourself, in the app. EU and UK users have rights under the GDPR, and California users under the CCPA. We do not sell or share personal information.

9. Privacy officer and contact

  • Privacy officer: Seongwook Cha (owner, STUDIO CHAAHN)
  • Email: contact@studiochaahn.com

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we announce it on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect (30 days for changes that are unfavourable to you). If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy first.
