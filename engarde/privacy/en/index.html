2. How we collect it

Data is created or collected automatically while you install, launch and play the game. Advertising data is collected by the ad SDKs included in the game when you view ads. Enquiry data is received when you email us.

3. Retention

  • We keep your data until you delete your account. See Account Deletion.
  • Data kept by law: records of payment and supply of goods for 5 years, and records of consumer complaints or disputes for 3 years (Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).
  • Operational logs (grants, revocations, sanctions) are stripped of your name after account deletion, kept by ID number only for 6 months, then deleted (for refunds and anti-cheat).
  • A minimal record that a deletion was processed (request and completion times) may remain.
  • Data processed by ad partners is retained under each partner's own privacy policy.

Account, game record, purchase and friend data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data exchanged during a real-time match (movement and attack inputs, and the name tag, player ID and equipped cosmetics shown to your opponent) may be sent unencrypted for low latency.

5. Ads and opting out of personalised ads

  • The game has rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and an interstitial ad shown before searching for a ranked or casual match every few matches. Buying "No ads" removes both.
  • In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and some US states), an ad consent form appears on first launch, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy choices" in the game settings.
  • On iOS, if you decline the tracking request we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking)
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • Refusing personalised ads does not reduce the number of ads; you may see less relevant ads.

6. Deletion

  • When you request account deletion, after a 30-day grace period (or immediately, if you choose), we erase your account, progress, leaderboard entries and friends data from our servers in a way that cannot be recovered.
  • Data we must keep by law is stored separately from other data and deleted without delay when the period ends.
  • Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

7. Your rights

You (or a legal guardian, for users under 14) can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information, and can withdraw consent. Use the game settings (account deletion, ad privacy choices) or email us at the address below. We will tell you the result within 10 days. EU and UK users have rights under the GDPR, and California users under the CCPA. We do not sell personal information. However, ad partners' collection for personalised ads may count as "sharing" under California law (CPRA), which you can refuse under "Ad privacy choices" in the game settings.

EU and UK users: the legal bases for processing are (1) performance of a contract (account, game records, friends, purchases, real-time matches), (2) legitimate interests (anti-cheat, handling reports, security), (3) consent (personalised ads, advertising ID) and (4) legal obligation (keeping payment records). You can withdraw consent at any time and have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority of your country.

7-2. Automated decisions

The following two decisions are made automatically, without human involvement.

  • Refund abuse restrictions: for refunds that meet the criteria in section 3.6 of the Terms of Service (refunds more than 7 days after purchase, refunds of items applied immediately, and refunds within 7 days from the fifth one in a year), the first restricts matches for 7 days and a second within the last year permanently suspends the account.
  • Match record verification: match inputs are recalculated, and if the result does not match, that result is not accepted.

You can ask for an explanation of these decisions, object to them and request human review (by email; Article 37-2 of the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, Article 22 GDPR). A person will review your case and reply.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 14. Age is checked on first launch and users under 14 cannot play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that such information was processed without a legal guardian's consent, we delete it without delay.

9. Automatic collection tools

The game does not use cookies. The ad SDKs use the advertising ID, which you can refuse as described in section 5.

10. Security measures

Server access is limited to the minimum necessary, and admin keys are stored separately and never included in the game app. Account, game record and purchase communications are encrypted (see section 4 for real-time match inputs). Payments are verified directly with the store servers, and we do not store payment details.
