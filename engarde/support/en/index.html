Contact us

Email contact@studiochaahn.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.

To help us check quickly, please include:

  • Your name tag (name#number, shown in the game settings)
  • Your device type and app version
  • What happened and roughly when, with a screenshot if possible
  • For purchase issues, your store order number (Google Play: GPA.…, App Store: the order ID on your receipt)

Frequently asked questions

I bought something but the item is missing
Connect to the internet and restart the game. If it's still missing, email us with the order number.

I want to move to another device
Link a Google or Apple account under Settings → Manage account, then sign in with the same account on the new device. If that doesn't work, email us with your name tag.

I want to delete my account
See Account Deletion.

I want a refund
Request it through the store you paid with. The conditions are in section 3 of the Terms of Service.

I saw an inappropriate name or bad behaviour
Tap "Report" on the match result screen. Our staff will review it.
