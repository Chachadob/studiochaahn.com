1. Account and age

  • The game is for players aged 14 and over. Age is checked on first launch.
  • You start with a guest account and can link a Google or Apple account. A guest account is tied to your device, so it may not be recoverable if you change devices or delete the app without linking an account. If you switch to an account already linked to other progress, your current guest progress does not carry over (paid items can be moved with "Restore purchases").
  • Paid items can be received again on your current game account with "Restore purchases" on the same store account. Restoring moves the items away from the game account that had them, and the same purchase can be moved only once every 24 hours.

2. Paid items

  1. Prices are the local prices shown in the store, and payments are processed by Google Play or the App Store. Every paid item is a permanent item that you buy once and keep using, and it can be used until the service ends. There are no random-reward (loot box) items.

  3. Items are lost when you delete your account. If you have unused items still within the withdrawal period, request a refund before deleting your account.

3. Withdrawal (refunds)

  1. How to request: as a rule, refunds are requested through the store you paid with (Google Play for Google Play purchases, reportaproblem.apple.com for App Store purchases). For a withdrawal the store did not process, or to cancel a purchase made by a minor, email contact@studiochaahn.com and we will handle it as the law requires.
  2. Withdrawal period: items not yet equipped or used can be withdrawn within 7 days of purchase. If an item differs from how it was described or advertised, or was delivered differently from the contract, you can withdraw within 3 months of receiving it or 30 days of learning of the difference.
  3. When withdrawal is limited (Article 17(2) of the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce): items that have been equipped or used, items whose effect applies at the moment of purchase (ad removal, technique unlocks), and bundles of which a part has been equipped or used. An item the game equips automatically on purchase does not count as equipped or used. This is shown in the purchase confirmation window and the shop screen, and techniques can be tried for free before purchase with "Try it".
  4. Purchases by minors: if a minor paid without the consent of a legal guardian, the minor or the guardian can cancel the purchase, except in cases defined by law, such as payments within the scope the guardian allowed.
  5. Refunded purchases: items delivered by a purchase that the store refunded are revoked from every account that received that purchase (an equipped item returns to the default). A refund of unequipped, unused items within 7 days of purchase is treated as a legitimate withdrawal and does not restrict your use.
  6. Abuse of payments and refunds: counting each refund below as one case, we treat it as payment or refund abuse; the first case restricts matches for 7 days, and a second case within the last year permanently suspends the account.
    • A refund made more than 7 days after purchase
    • A refund of an item applied at the moment of purchase (ad removal, technique unlocks, or a bundle containing them)
    • Refunds within 7 days, from the fifth one in a year, which we treat as repeated buying and refunding

While restricted or suspended, you can still use the shop, restore purchases, delete your account and contact us. These decisions are made automatically, and the reason is shown in the game. If you appeal, a person reviews the case and lifts the restriction if it was mistaken.

If no opponent is found within a certain time in a ranked match, a computer-controlled fencer (AI) of similar skill may be assigned as your opponent, and the result of that match also counts toward your rank points (RP). Computer fencers do not appear on leaderboards.
